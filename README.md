# 🏠 Aotearoa Is Home

**A web-based support platform for international students and their families settling into life in New Zealand.**

## 📖 About the Project

**Aotearoa Is Home** is a web-based application designed to support international students and their families as they settle into **New Zealand culture and society**.

The project aims to improve the settlement experience of newly arrived international students and their families by providing **reliable, accessible, and personalised information** to support their wellbeing, community engagement, and social integration during the early stages of their journey in Aotearoa New Zealand.

The initial target audience is international students and their families associated with **Whitireia and WelTec**. However, the application has been designed with the potential to expand its services to support international students across New Zealand in the future.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of **Aotearoa Is Home** are to:

* Support international students and their families during their settlement journey in New Zealand.
* Provide reliable and accessible information about life in Aotearoa.
* Support students' and families' physical and mental wellbeing.
* Encourage community engagement and social integration.
* Provide information about community, Whitireia, and WelTec events.
* Enable users to discover and participate in community activities and meet-up opportunities.
* Provide an event calendar to help users stay informed about upcoming activities.
* Provide notification features for relevant events and information.
* Contribute to a positive student experience and strengthen the reputation of Whitireia and WelTec through improved student wellbeing and positive word-of-mouth.
* Provide a foundation that can potentially be expanded to support international students throughout New Zealand.

## 👥 Target Users

The application supports multiple user roles:

| User Role                | Purpose                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🎓 International Student | Access settlement information, events, community opportunities and support resources |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Member   | Access information and resources relevant to supporting international students       |
| 🛠️ Administrator        | Manage users, content, events and application functionality                          |
| 📅 Event Provider        | Create and manage community or campus event information                              |

## 🔄 Development Methodology

The project team follows the **Scrum Agile methodology**.

Development is organised into **four sprints**, with each sprint involving activities such as:

1. Sprint Planning
2. Requirement Analysis
3. Design and Development
4. Testing
5. Sprint Review
6. Feedback and Improvements

The project is managed using **Trello**, while **GitHub** is used for version control and collaborative development.
